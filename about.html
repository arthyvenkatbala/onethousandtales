Who is Dilip Kumar

Observation over direction.

Founder · One Thousand Tales · Chennai

Over 14 years, I've come to believe that the most important photographs happen when nobody is looking at the camera.

I'm not interested in directing people. I'm interested in what they reveal unintentionally — the expressions that surface between the planned moments, the relationships that appear in the background of an image that was meant to capture something else.

Photography, for me, is a practice of paying attention to things most people walk past.

The Studio

A Studio Built Around Attention.

One Thousand Tales is a wedding and family photography studio based in Chennai, founded and led by Dilip Kumar. For over fourteen years, the studio has photographed weddings, couples, and families the way they actually happen — not staged, not directed, but observed.

The work falls into a few related forms. Wedding photography sits at the centre of it — full days and multi-event celebrations, photographed candidly as they unfold, with documentary wedding photography as the guiding approach rather than a checklist of poses. Family photography and couple sessions sit alongside this, built on the same idea: the most honest images come from people who've stopped performing for the camera. When couples ask us to travel — for a destination wedding outside Chennai, elsewhere in India, or further still — we go, carrying the same approach wherever the celebration is held.

Chennai shapes a lot of how this studio sees a wedding. Tamil weddings carry their own rhythm — the muhurtham at dawn, a hall filling with relatives, rituals that move quickly and rarely pause for a photographer. Working here for over a decade means knowing when to stay still and let a moment happen, rather than interrupting it to set one up.

Why photograph weddings this way? Because a wedding day is unrepeatable, and most of what makes it meaningful isn't the choreographed part — it's the look between two people mid-ceremony, a parent's hand on a shoulder, the five minutes before someone walks down the aisle when nobody is performing. A posed photograph can be recreated. A candid one can't. That difference is the reason this studio exists in its current form: less direction, more attention.

One Thousand Tales isn't a large team running packages and add-ons. It's a smaller, more personal practice — one photographer, one perspective, applied consistently across every wedding, every family, every story photographed in Chennai and beyond. That's also why the name fits: every couple's wedding is one more tale worth telling properly.
