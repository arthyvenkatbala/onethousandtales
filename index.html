Our Philosophy

Why One Thousand Tales?

01

Observation over Direction

We never direct people or create polished emotional scenes. We observe. The best moments happen when no one is watching the camera.

02

Emotion in its Natural State

Some photographs are emotional. Some are chaotic. Some are funny without trying to be. We don't push emotions — we catch them in the wild.

03

Images That Stay Alive

A gesture in the background. A relationship hidden within the frame. A detail that once felt invisible. Our images continue revealing new things over time.

04

Everyone in the Frame

One Thousand Tales was built around the entire story — where everyone becomes part of the frame, not just the couple at the centre.

About

Dilip Kumar

Photographer · Chennai

Over 14 years, I've come to believe that the most important photographs happen when nobody is looking at the camera.

I'm not interested in directing people. I'm interested in what they reveal unintentionally — the expressions that surface between the planned moments, the relationships that appear in the background of an image that was meant to capture something else.

Photography, for me, is a practice of paying attention to things most people walk past.
